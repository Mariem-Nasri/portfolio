# Free project scene image credits

All photographs are bundled locally so the portfolio does not depend on third-party image URLs. The pipeline close-ups are representative reference photos, not footage or claimed detections from the project.

## Pipeline inspection

The portfolio panel now uses the refinery photograph supplied in `drone_vid/public/images/refinery.jpg`. The moving drone and detection overlays are a visual demo; they do not represent recorded project output. The listed pipeline reference photographs below are retained locally but are no longer displayed in the drone panel.

- `pipeline-base.jpg` — “Pipeline in the Mojave Desert, California,” Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA), U.S. Department of Transportation. U.S. federal government work; public domain in the United States. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pipeline_in_the_Mojave_Desert,_California.jpg).
- `pipeline-crack.jpg` — “Photo of ruptured segment of pipeline…,” National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB). U.S. federal government work; public domain in the United States. [Source and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Photo_of_ruptured_segment_of_pipeline_from_Enbridge_Incorporated_Hazardous_Liquid_Pipeline_Rupture_and_Release_Marshall,_Michigan_July_25,_2010_%2833428475430%29.jpg).
